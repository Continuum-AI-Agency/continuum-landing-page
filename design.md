# Continuum — Landing design language

**Scope:** public site only (trycontinuum.ai and related marketing).  
**Not in scope:** the product app. The app stays matte, light, and consistent. Landscapes, planets, and climate art never enter app chrome.

This file replaces the public face of Continuum. The product UI contract still governs the tool.

---

## 1. Split

Two registers. Do not mix them.

| Register | Where | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **World** | Landing, feature films, launch stills | Tell the factory story through places |
| **Tool** | App, and any product window shown on the site | Look exactly like Continuum as shipped |

The site is weather. The product is a quiet window sitting in that weather.

If a visual exists only to signal “AI,” remove it.

---

## 2. Brand idea

Continuum is a creative factory for paid creative.

Work travels. It lands. The next cycle starts from what just happened.

The landing page makes that physical: **one planet, several locations on it.** Each factory station is a climate you can stand in. Not separate brands. Not a space opera. Terrestrial places on a single world (and its moon).

Hero establishes the planet from space / high atmosphere.  
Every other still is a location **on** that world.

---

## 3. Artistic style (all world art)

Match the first reference still (*Introducing Agentic Search* — red canyon).

**Digital environmental concept art**

- Painterly digital brushwork. Visible strokes. Analog grain.
- Atmospheric depth and dramatic scale.
- No HUD, no UI chrome, no spacecraft as the subject, no people, no logos, no type burned into the plate.
- Feels produced, not default-generated.

**How to judge a plate**

- Could it hang next to the red canyon still? Same hand, same grain, different climate.
- Is it a place you could walk? If it only works as a galaxy wallpaper, it is wrong.
- Is the color a geology, not a gradient overlay?

**Do not use**

- Neon, glow rims, cyber grids, glass cards as the world
- Photoreal NASA stills
- Cute mascots
- Black-hole jewelry / event-horizon logos
- Purple–cyan “AI” identity washes on the sky

---

## 4. The planet

One unnamed world. Continuum’s public geography.

Locations are biomes of that world. The moon is part of the same system: a place you can stand, looking back at the ringed planet.

Factory stations map to climates. The mapping is subliminal — a visitor should feel a change of weather, not read a theme-park label.

## 7. Type

**World display:** large, tight, humanist geometric. White on paintings.

**Product / UI:** Geist Sans. Geist Mono for station indexes, IDs, substitutions, `STEP 03 / 06`.

**On the landing page**

- H1 can sit in the hero air
- Do not gradient the type
- Do not put display type in the product window
- Mono for factory indexing only

---

## 8. Motion

Allowed on the landing page, restrained.

- Dots may gather into a shape (loop, frame, station), then rest. They are condensate / matter, not stars and not confetti.
- Climate crossfades when the loop station changes.
- Product window does not bounce in. It is already there.
- A transfer between stations may be a brief lens in air — not an accretion disk.

If removing the motion does not reduce understanding, it is optional. Respect reduced-motion.

---

## 9. Voice (landing copy)

Continuum is a creative factory.

**Use:** Connect, analyze, recommend, generate, render, publish, sync, write back, review.

**Do not use:** AI Marketing Agency, magic, unleash, revolutionize, “our AI.”

Name the parts. Optimizer kicks. Continuum analyzes. Jaina reasons through brand guidelines. AEP renders only if a new production asset is required. Write-back closes the loop.

**Hero lines to test (pick one)**

1. Continuum — A production line for ads that get better.  
2. Continuum — A creative factory for paid creative.  
3. Signal in. Asset live. Loop closed.

**Line to hold**

Continuum is not an AI ad generator.  
It is a creative factory: the software-factory idea applied to paid creative.  
If nothing writes back into the account, the loop is open.

## 10. Landing page map

Replace the current arc (Agency → OS → four stages → Social+ / Studio+ / Performance+ → empty metrics).

1. **Hero — Orbit**  
   Kicker `CREATIVE FACTORY`. H1 Continuum. Product window in `#fdfdfd` on the planet. CTAs: Book a demo / See the loop.

2. **The line to hold**  
   Three sentences. No decoration required.

3. **The jump**  
   Four substitutions: ticket, analysis, build, write-back. Software column quiet. Creative column live.

4. **The loop**  
   Named parts. Climate still changes with the station. Do not collapse Optimizer / Continuum / Jaina / Generate / AEP / Account.

5. **Worlds**  
   Location plates as section weather. Short caption. Not a feature grid of plus-products.

6. **Why it holds**  
   Reporting tools vs generation tools vs the factory.

7. **Close**  
   Orbit or Harbor. “The next ad should already know what happened.” Book a demo.

Founders and pricing are not hero slots. Social+ / Studio+ / Performance+ are not the sitemap.

## 11. Product window on the site

When the landing page shows the app, it must be indistinguishable in temperature from the real app.

- Ground `#fdfdfd`
- Rail `#f9f8ff`
- Rules `#e4e2f7`
- Type `#16162a`
- Unloaded rows as quiet gray bars
- Violet only as selected station / primary control

Do not restyle the mock to match the desert. The contrast is the brand: rich place outside, quiet instrument inside.

## 13. Art production checklist

For each commissioned still:

- [ ] Painterly digital brushwork, visible grain  
- [ ] Reads as a place on the Continuum planet (or its moon)  
- [ ] Same hand as the red canyon reference  
- [ ] No type, figures, craft-as-subject, or UI  
- [ ] Safe area for type or a product window  
- [ ] Palette matches its location brief  
- [ ] Works cropped square and 16:9  
- [ ] Does not require neon to feel “sci-fi”

**First drop:** Orbit, Survey range, Moon shore, Reef.

---

## 14. Governance

1. Product UI does not adopt planets or climates as chrome.  
2. Landing does not adopt navy-aurora or agency copy.  
3. A product screenshot on the site uses product tokens, not world color.  
4. Climate is allowed in hero, section horizons, loop stage, and close.  
5. If a historical rule conflicts with the shipped app, the shipped app wins for the tool; this file wins for the public world.
